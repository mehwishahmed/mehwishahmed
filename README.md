### [Criteo Ad Incrementality](https://github.com/mehwishahmed/criteo-ad-incrementality)
Causal analysis of a 1.4M-user sample of Criteo's randomized ad experiment. Caught a pre-treatment imbalance and corrected for it.

- Lin adjustment revised lift **27.9% → 20.2%** (**+0.81 pp**, 95% CI 0.73–0.89), confirmed with AIPW
- **+23 pp** effect on exposed users via instrumental variables
- XGBoost uplift model: top 20% of users capture **72%** of incremental visits

`Causal Inference` `A/B Testing` `XGBoost`
